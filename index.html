Completion Checklists

Secret Ending Route

  • Complete Ancient Guanyin Temple in Chapter 1.
  • Complete Kingdom of Sahali in Chapter 2.
  • Finish the Treasure Hunter quest in Chapter 3.
  • Complete Purple Cloud Mountain in Chapter 4.
  • Complete Bishui Cave in Chapter 5.
  • Return to the Great Pagoda and pursue Mount Mei before starting a New Cycle.

Four Vessels

  • Fireproof Mantle — Chapter 1 secret area.
  • Wind Tamer — Chapter 2 secret area.
  • Weaver's Needle — Chapter 4 secret area.
  • Plantain Fan — Chapter 5 story reward.

High-Risk Missables

  • Exhaust Horse Guai and Old Man dialogue in every relevant chapter before moving on.
  • Deal with Wandering Wight before resolving the Chapter 1 bell-secret path.
  • Break Venom Daoist's extra arms during the first Chapter 4 fight for Venomous Armguard.
  • Finish Purple Talismans, Daoist Mi and other Purple Cloud Mountain content before Chapter 4's finale.
  • Do not choose “Enter a New Cycle” until cleanup is finished.

NPC Priority List

  • Horse Guai (Ma Tianba) — exhaust dialogue Chapter 1 through 5; finish his cart interaction after Chapter 5.
  • Old Man / Yuan Shoucheng — speak whenever he appears before advancing chapters.
  • Shen Monkey — Gourd, Drink and Soak progression.
  • Xu Dog — medicines and Celestial Medicines.
  • Chen Loong — unlocks Zodiac Village through the Ruyi Scroll quest.
  • Yin Tiger — armor upgrades, Iron-Tough armor and Ebon Flow.

Important Armor / Gear

  • Pilgrim set — useful early mobility/exploration option.
  • Iron-Tough set — unlocked through Yin Tiger in Zodiac Village.
  • Venomous Armguard — missable Chapter 4 boss-part break reward.
  • Bull King set — Bishui Cave reward path; full set requires NG+ materials.
  • Wukong mythical set — four Chapter 6 boss rewards used before claiming Jingubang.
  • Fireproof Mantle, Wind Tamer, Weaver's Needle and Plantain Fan — major Vessel milestones.

Four Loongs

  • Get Loong Scales from the hidden wall in Chapter 2.
  • Red Loong — revisit Chapter 1.
  • Black Loong — Chapter 2, Rockrest Flat.
  • Cyan Loong — Chapter 3, Turtle Island.
  • Yellow Loong — Chapter 4 after the first three; rewards Golden Lining and Golden Carp.

Unique One-Run Missables

  • Auspicious Lantern — kill all 9 Lantern Wardens before Captain Wise-Voice.
  • Apramana Bat — defeat while Zhu Bajie is still with you at Bitter Lake.
  • Venomous Armguard — break Venom Daoist's extra arms in his first fight.
  • Stained Jade Gourd — defeat Scorpionlord before Duskveil.
  • Violet Hail — complete Daoist Mi before Duskveil.

Five Skandhas

  • Form — Chapter 1.
  • Feeling — Chapter 2, from Shigandang.
  • Thought — Chapter 3.
  • Choice — Chapter 4.
  • Consciousness — Chapter 6, from Giant Shigandang.
  • Give all five to Xu Dog for the Five Skandhas Pill.

Cleanup Before NG+

  • Finish Journal portraits, Curios, Gourds, Drinks, Soaks, Spirits and Transformations you care about.
  • Finish remaining side quests and hidden bosses.
  • Use Keeper's Shrines to revisit earlier chapters.
  • Export this checklist if you want a backup.

⚡ Power Route

Strong, flexible progression — respec whenever a boss calls for it.

Default Build: Safe & Strong

  • Stance: Smash. Build Focus through Light attacks, then cash it out with charged Heavy / Resolute-style punishes.
  • Spells: Immobilize + Cloud Step for most of the game.
  • Transformation: Red Tides early; use transformations proactively as a safe damage phase.
  • Spirit: Wandering Wight is a simple high-impact early option; land it after a stagger or Immobilize.
  • Stats: enough stamina/HP to avoid dying to one mistake, then shift toward Attack and Crit.

Boss-Delete Variant

  • Unlock Spell Binder in Chapter 3.
  • Respec out of spell investments you cannot use while Spell Binder is active.
  • Move those Sparks into your stance, Martial Arts, Attack/Crit and useful Foundation nodes.
  • Use this when you already know the boss well enough to survive without Cloud Step / Immobilize.
  • Switch back freely at a shrine if the matchup punishes pure melee.

Vessel Matchups

  • Fireproof Mantle: Black Bear Guai / fire-heavy encounters.
  • Wind Tamer: save for Yellow Wind Sage's wind pressure.
  • Weaver's Needle: activate during Hundred-Eyed Daoist Master's yellow-domain phase.
  • Plantain Fan: premier choice against Erlang's shield.

Spend Materials Efficiently

  • Do not upgrade every armor set. Pick one set whose bonus you actively use and keep it current.
  • Upgrade your Gourd/Drink setup whenever Shen Monkey offers meaningful improvements.
  • Use Xu Dog's Celestial Medicines to fix a real weakness rather than spreading Mind Cores evenly.
  • Save rare crafting materials until you know which weapon/armor path fits your build.

Help

Is this a full walkthrough?

No. It is intentionally formatted like a playthrough cheat sheet: short actionable reminders instead of long route explanations.

Do I have to follow this exact order?

No. Black Myth: Wukong allows revisiting earlier chapters from Keeper's Shrines, and many optional encounters can be done in different orders.

Can I track multiple runs?

Yes. Use Add/Edit in the header to create profiles for different characters or NG+ cycles.

Can I back up progress?

Yes. Export downloads a JSON backup. Import restores that file into this browser.

What do the gold linked names do?

They open the matching Black Myth: Wukong community wiki detail page directly in a new tab for that boss, NPC, item, armor piece, spell, Spirit, Curio or location.
