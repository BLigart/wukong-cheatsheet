Completion Guide

What this route covers

A completion companion for the base campaign and NG+. Generic loot chests and post-launch challenge rewards are outside this route. Optional Deluxe equipment is shown in Audit but excluded from its totals. This fan guide is not a guarantee that every interaction is documented.

  • Every boss / boss-type encounter, including optional and secret encounters.
  • Quest NPC chains and chapter-specific dialogue that can lock out rewards.
  • All major secret areas, Four Loongs, Five Skandhas, Vessels and true-ending route.
  • Completion-relevant gear systems: Spirits, Curios, armor, weapons, transformations, Gourds/Drinks/Soaks, meditation spots, upgrade materials and NG+ requirements.

What “100%” means here

This is aimed at full journal/trophy/achievement-style completion and unique equipment. It does not force you to open every generic Will chest, collect every common crafting material, or farm duplicate copies of equipment.

RNG drops are placed at sensible farming checkpoints. You can postpone them to Chapter 6 cleanup if you prefer.

Ending order

If you want to see both ending cutscenes on one save, finish the normal final-boss sequence once, choose Continue Journey, then complete Mount Mei / Erlang and defeat the final boss again for the secret ending. Do all cleanup before starting a New Cycle.

Power guide · first playthrough

Become OP: build one engine, not three half-builds

My recommended power route is Smash Stance + the full Pilgrim Armor Set. The set stacks Attack while sprinting; at Mythical it also generates Focus while sprinting. Force Unbound lets Smash charge Heavy Attacks while sprinting, so one action simultaneously keeps you mobile, builds the armor buff, charges Focus and prepares your nuke.

Main stance: SMASHMain armor: PILGRIMSafe spells: IMMOBILIZE + CLOUD STEPEarly Spirit: WANDERING WIGHTBoss-delete swap: SPELL BINDER
Ch. 1–2Build the core

Craft full Pilgrim early. Put Sparks into Foundation + Smash, not all three stances. Use Immobilize, Red Tides and Wandering Wight. Your goal is a reliable 2–3 Focus sprint-charged Heavy.

Ch. 3Power spike

Unlock Zodiac Village / Yin Tiger and start upgrading Pilgrim. Use the Cloud Step and A Pluck of Many spells learned in Chapters 1–2. Finish Treasure Hunter for Spell Binder and keep it as your “I know this boss” damage mode.

After YellowbrowUnlock the Qi alternative

Craft the Golden Armor Set if you want Vessel/Spirit spam. Its set bonus boosts Attack after a Spirit/Vessel and critical hits restore Qi, making it a strong control build.

Ch. 4–5Commit materials

If Smash + Pilgrim feels right, stop upgrading random sets and push all four Pilgrim pieces toward Mythical. Rare materials are more valuable concentrated into one complete build.

Ch. 6Two endgame loadouts

Use Jingubang + Wukong armor as the effortless story baseline, but keep Mythical Pilgrim for sprint-Smash burst. Keep Golden Armor + Plantain Fan ready for shield/control-heavy fights such as Erlang.

NG+Turn the dial further

Full Bull King gear becomes possible with more Iron Horns. High-crit NG+ weapons also make Spell Binder and Golden Qi builds increasingly absurd.

1. Sparks — what to invest in

1

Foundation first: buy stamina / dodge-efficiency quality-of-life such as Simian Agility. Enough Stamina to finish a combo and still dodge is worth more than a second half-built stance.

2

Smash next: prioritize Force Unbound, Resolute Counterflow, Winding Wind and Skyfall Strike. Build one stance deeply before buying luxury nodes in Pillar/Thrust.

3

Immobilize: Crash is an early damage priority; add Evanescence if you consistently time casts into attacks.

4

Cloud Step: invest when bosses become fast enough that raw dodging costs you damage uptime. It is your safest way to heal, reposition, charge and start a burst window.

5

A Pluck of Many: do not rush the entire tree while your Mana pool is tiny. Once Mana is comfortable, clones + Immobilize can erase large chunks of a boss health bar.

Avoid: distributing Sparks evenly across Smash, Pillar and Thrust. You can respec freely at Keeper’s Shrines, so specialize for the fight instead of permanently hedging.

2. Default “OP but safe” loadout

  • Stance: Smash Stance.
  • Mysticism: Immobilize.
  • Alteration: Cloud Step for safety. Swap to Rock Solid only if you enjoy parry timing.
  • Strand: A Pluck of Many once you can afford its Mana cost.
  • Spirit: upgrade Wandering Wight Spirit; its fast stagger/burst remains useful for a long time.
  • Transformation: Red Tides early. Treat a Transformation like a second health/damage phase instead of saving it forever.
  • Vessel: use the chapter counter-vessel when relevant; by late game Plantain Fan is outstanding for creating long openings.

Combat loop: sprint while charging Smash → let Pilgrim Attack stacks build → create an opening with Immobilize / Cloud Step / Spirit → cash out the charged Heavy → rebuild Focus while staying mobile.

3. Armor — what to wear and upgrade

Chapter 1–2: Pilgrim

Craft all four pieces as soon as practical and keep the full-set bonus active. Do not waste rare upgrade materials on every new armor set just because its Defense number is higher.

Chapter 3: start upgrading it

After unlocking Yin Tiger, upgrade the Pilgrim set if you have committed to Smash. Bringing all four pieces to Mythical is the real target because Mythical unlocks Focus generation while sprinting.

After Yellowbrow: Golden as your second set

The full Golden Armor Set is the best alternate set to keep ready. Spirit/Vessel use temporarily increases Attack and critical hits restore Qi. It is excellent when repeated control is more valuable than one enormous Heavy.

Chapter 6: do not automatically throw Pilgrim away

The Wukong mythical set + Jingubang is a very strong plug-and-play finale setup. But a completed Mythical Pilgrim set still has a unique sprint/Focus engine, so switch based on the boss rather than rarity color.

Material rule: fully upgrading one synergistic set is usually stronger than owning three half-upgraded sets. Save Celestial Ribbons until you know which endgame loop you actually enjoy.

4. Spell Binder — boss delete mode

After the Green-Capped Martialist quest in Chapter 3, Spell Binder converts your Mana investment into Attack, but while active it locks every other Spell, Vessel, Transformation and Spirit Skill.

  • Respec Sparks out of spell trees you cannot use and put them into Smash, Martial Arts, Foundation and survivability.
  • Max Mana becomes an offensive stat, so Celestial Medicine investment in Mana gains extra value for this loadout.
  • Use your highest-Attack staff, Pilgrim sprint stacks and charged Smash Heavy attacks.
  • Use this on bosses whose patterns you already understand. If you are still learning, Cloud Step + Immobilize usually produces more real DPS because you stay alive.

When it feels broken: you no longer spend Mana during the fight, and Spell Binder turns that unused resource pool into raw martial damage for every hit.

5. Golden Qi build — control / Erlang mode

Use this when a boss has a shield, long phases or keeps denying your charged Heavy setup.

  • Armor: full Golden Armor Set.
  • Vessel: Plantain Fan.
  • Spirit: Wandering Wight for fast burst/stagger or Apramana Bat Spirit when Chill control works.
  • Stats/gear: lean into Critical Hit Chance because Golden’s full-set effect returns Qi on crits.
  • During openings: rapid-hit Staff Spin can generate many crit opportunities, rebuilding Qi faster for the next Fan/Spirit cycle.

Loop: Vessel/Spirit → gain Golden attack buff → unload while the boss is controlled → crits rebuild Qi → repeat. Against Erlang, save burst until his shield is being stripped rather than wasting it into full protection.

6. “Am I spending resources correctly?”

  • Sparks: Foundation → one stance → one reliable spell package. Respec when the matchup changes.
  • Mind Cores: normal build = enough Health/Stamina to survive mistakes; Spell Binder build = prioritize Max Mana much more aggressively.
  • Armor materials: upgrade one main set; keep a second specialized set only if it solves a real matchup.
  • Spirit upgrades: spend on a Spirit you actually press every boss fight instead of leveling ten mediocre options.
  • Consumables: use them on hard bosses. Hoarding medicines until the credits is the opposite of becoming OP.

Most common power mistake: equipping the newest/highest-rarity item without checking whether its passive and set bonus support your actual combat loop.

Build recommendations are a practical first-playthrough power route, not a claim that one setup is mathematically best for every boss. The app keeps free-respec alternatives because Wukong rewards matchup-specific swaps.
